        Privacy Policy
        
          
        
          
        We run no servers and operate no backend. There is no ats-fill account, and your data is never sent to us — we have no way to receive, store, or see it.
        
          
          
            
        • What is stored, and where. Your profile, resume preview, saved answer defaults ("memory"), application tracker, settings, and any keys/credentials you add are stored only in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) on this device/profile. Nothing syncs to the cloud.
          • 
            
        • What leaves your device. Data leaves only when you trigger an optional network action: AI help (resume parsing, answer drafting, JD summarize/clean-up) sends the relevant text directly from your browser to Google's Gemini API using your own key; Job Search sends your search query to whichever sources you have enabled (keyless boards, your keyed sources, or any custom RSS source you've added); LinkedIn/Google profile import sends an OAuth handshake to that provider using your own app credentials. Without a Gemini key, autofill is fully on-device and sends nothing.
          • 
            
        • Sensitive / demographic fields (race, gender, veteran, disability) are opt-in, stored locally only, and are never included in AI requests.
          • 
            
        • Third parties. Only the services above can receive your data, and only for requests you explicitly initiate with your own credentials.
          • 
            
        • No tracking. No analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry of any kind.
          • 
          
        
          
        
        
        

        
        
          
        
            
        GDPR & CCPA / your rights
        
            
        
            
        Because all data lives locally on your device and we never receive it, you remain the sole controller of your information and can exercise every right directly from this panel:
        
            
          
              
        • Access & portability: Export your tracker to CSV from the Pipeline screen; your profile and memory are visible and editable in the Profile screen.
          • 
              
        • Rectification: Edit any saved field at any time in Profile.
          • 
              
        • Erasure ("right to be forgotten"): Use Delete all local data below to wipe everything instantly. No request to us is needed because we hold nothing.
          • 
              
        • Withdraw consent: Re-open privacy settings to review or revoke. Deleting local data clears your recorded consent.

        What is an ATS?
        
            
        
            
        An applicant tracking system is the hiring software behind many job forms. ats-fill uses ATS detection to decide how safely it can fill fields and how much review to keep in the loop.
        
            
        
          
        
        
        

        
        
          
        
            
        Data controls — what each action removes
        
            
        
            
        
              
        🧹 Clear temp cache
        
              
        Removes only short-lived working data and keeps everything you set up. Specifically it deletes: cached page drafts (in-progress form values), the last generated answer set, the last fill report, and the "recently filled" pointer. Your profile, resume, saved memory, tracker, settings, and Gemini key are kept.
        
            
        
            
        
              
        🗑 Delete all local data
        
              
        Wipes everything this extension stores on your device — profile and resume, saved answer defaults & ignore list, the entire application tracker, all settings including your Gemini API key and your recorded consent, plus everything the temp cache covers. This cannot be undone.
